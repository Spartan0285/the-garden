The Garden
==========

Software, pictures and descriptions come from the Macintosh Garden
(https://macintoshgarden.org), a community archive. The Garden is an
independent client and is not affiliated with it.

The Garden is free software under the MIT license. The full license texts of
The Garden and of the components below are in the Licenses folder next to
this file.

Archive support: XADMaster and UniversalDetector
------------------------------------------------
The Garden unpacks archives with XADMaster, the engine of The Unarchiver
by Dag Agren (https://theunarchiver.com), and UniversalDetector. They are
included unmodified, as the dynamic frameworks shipped with The Unarchiver
3.11.1, in The Garden.app/Contents/Frameworks, under the GNU Lesser General
Public License version 2.1 (https://www.gnu.org/licenses/lgpl-2.1.html).
You may replace them with your own builds of the same frameworks. Source:
https://github.com/MacPaw/XADMaster and
https://github.com/MacPaw/universal-detector

Networking: libcurl (curl license), OpenSSL 3 (Apache License 2.0),
zlib (zlib license), all statically linked.
